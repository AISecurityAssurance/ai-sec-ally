## **Cybersecurity Research Engineer**

**Annual base salary: $140,000 - $160,000**

*Remote / U.S.-Based | Anticipated full-time W-2 employment*

### **The Opportunity - Please Read First**

AI Security Assurance is recruiting in anticipation of a government-funded research and development contract. **Position availability and the start date depend on contract award and funding.** We are beginning conversations now with people interested in the work and available to join if the contract is awarded. There is no guarantee of award or employment.

### **About Us**

AI Security Assurance is a remote-first startup developing automated tools for systems security and safety assurance. Our research connects source code and binary behavior to higher-level models so that interactions, security weaknesses, and their consequences can be examined across a system. We combine systems-theoretic analysis, formal methods, and AI-assisted engineering, with evidence and human judgment guiding the results.

### **The Role**

You will bring deep cybersecurity and reverse-engineering knowledge to research on recovering software behavior and evaluating automated analysis. Your judgment will help the team determine what tools can establish, where they fail, and what evidence is needed to trust a finding.

Working with our AI engineers, software developers, and systems-assurance researchers, you will:

* Analyze source code and binaries to recover behavior, identify vulnerabilities, and explain the conditions that trigger them.
* Evaluate reverse-engineering and program-analysis tools, investigating incorrect, incomplete, or ambiguous results.
* Help design and review reference cases and datasets that support reproducible evaluation of recovery and security-analysis methods.
* Define technical requirements and experiments for analysis tools, and work with the team to build, adapt, and validate them using AI assistance where useful.
* Connect low-level findings to higher-level models while preserving actual behavior, including defects, dependencies, state, and ordering.
* Explain findings and uncertainty clearly, review technical work, and help other team members develop sound analysis practices.

### **What We're Looking For**

We value demonstrated depth and sound judgment, with no fixed degree or years-of-experience requirement. You should be able to investigate difficult problems independently and explain how you validated your conclusions.

**Must-haves:**

* In-depth, hands-on reverse engineering and binary-analysis experience, supported by work you can explain in detail.
* Vulnerability-research experience: understanding a weakness, its triggering conditions, and how to validate its security implications.
* Strong low-level knowledge of C or C++, assembly, memory, and software or firmware internals; practical experience with debuggers and tools such as Ghidra, IDA, or Binary Ninja.
* Practical static and/or dynamic analysis skills, with the critical judgment to recognize misleading tool output and test competing explanations.
* Ability to read and reason about code, use scripting or programming to support investigations, and guide and validate tools developed with teammates and AI assistance.
* Ability to plan reproducible experiments, document evidence and limitations, and collaborate across disciplines.
* Ability to work in the U.S. without sponsorship; willingness/ability to obtain a U.S. security clearance for future government work.

**Nice-to-haves:**

* Fuzzing, firmware analysis, compiler or decompiler knowledge, or contributions to program-analysis tools.
* Experience evaluating AI-assisted analysis or developing research datasets and benchmarks.
* Exposure to STPA/STPA-Sec, systems modeling, formal methods, or connecting software vulnerabilities to system-level weaknesses.
