## **Founding Member: Cybersecurity Research Engineer**

*(Remote / U.S.-Based — founding-member role)*

### **About Us**

AI Security Assurance is an early-stage, remote-first startup on a mission to accelerate the delivery of trustworthy systems — systems proven secure and safe by design. We build automated tooling for whole-system security and safety assurance: it analyzes a system as it's actually built — including the interactions between components, where the most damaging design-level flaws hide — and produces the evidence that trustworthy systems require. Our work is grounded in Systems-Theoretic Process Analysis (STPA) and formal methods for security, following the NIST systems security engineering framework (SP 800-160). We're bootstrapped and pre-funding, building toward funded government R&D (DARPA, SBIR) and a commercial platform. We're assembling a founding team — people who want skin in the game.

### **The Opportunity — please read first**

This is a **founding-member role, not a salaried job today.** We're looking for someone to help us build the company and win the funded research contracts that pay for the work. In place of a salary now, founding members share in the upside through **equity / ownership participation**; when we land a contract that funds the role, that contract compensates the work. We're honest about the risk — there is no guarantee of funding — and we're looking for people who want to build something real and share in its success.

### **The Role**

As a founding **Cybersecurity Research Engineer**, you'll lead the research that grounds our tooling in real systems — reverse engineering and binary analysis, vulnerability research, and recovering a system's true design from source and binaries so it can be analyzed at the system level. You'll connect low-level findings back to design-level security analysis, and help shape the research behind our proposals.

### **What We're Looking For**

**Must-haves:**

* Vulnerability research experience (discovering and analyzing real weaknesses)
* Reverse engineering and binary analysis
* Strong low-level skills — e.g., C/assembly, debuggers, disassemblers (Ghidra/IDA/Binary Ninja), and software/firmware internals
* Static and/or dynamic analysis of binaries and source
* Ability to work independently and wear multiple hats in an early-stage team
* Ability to work in the U.S. without sponsorship; willingness/ability to obtain a U.S. security clearance for future government work

**Nice-to-haves:**

* AI / LLM familiarity and modern development tools (helpful, not the core job)
* Fuzzing, exploit development, or program-analysis tooling
* STPA-Sec or threat-modeling exposure; mapping vulnerabilities to design weaknesses
* Research, publications, CTF results, or published CVEs
